## Full description

WeParlay brings live sports schedules, scores, social challenges, and daily prediction tournaments together in one place.

Follow selected games with verified score updates, make team-versus-team predictions, create or accept custom WeParlay Cash challenges, and track your tournament entries and results. Challenges and tournament entries are recorded with clear histories, result verification, refunds for eligible voided events, and payout records.

Some features require an eligible account and may be unavailable by location, tier, or pending compliance requirements. Live video is available only through authorized providers and eligible access plans. Real-money, debit-card, crypto, and withdrawal features must remain unavailable until WeParlay has completed licensing, identity, age, location, responsible-gaming, payment, and jurisdiction requirements.

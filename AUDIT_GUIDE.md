## 🚨 Incident Response Plan

### Security Breach Protocol
1. **Immediate Response**: Isolate affected systems
2. **Assessment**: Determine breach scope and impact
3. **Notification**: Alert relevant authorities and users
4. **Mitigation**: Implement containment measures
5. **Recovery**: Restore secure operations

### Compliance Violation Response
1. **Investigation**: Determine violation nature and scope
2. **Documentation**: Record all relevant details
3. **Remediation**: Implement corrective measures
4. **Reporting**: Notify regulatory authorities
5. **Prevention**: Update policies and procedures

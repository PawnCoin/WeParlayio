
# Team Logos Download Instructions

To populate this folder with professional team logos, organize by sport/league:

## Folder Structure:
```
teams/
├── nba/           # All 30 NBA teams
├── nfl/           # All 32 NFL teams  
├── mlb/           # All 30 MLB teams
├── nhl/           # All 32 NHL teams
├── soccer/
│   ├── premier-league/  # 20 Premier League teams
│   ├── la-liga/         # 20 La Liga teams
│   ├── serie-a/         # 20 Serie A teams
│   ├── bundesliga/      # 18 Bundesliga teams
│   ├── ligue-1/         # 20 Ligue 1 teams
│   └── mls/             # 30 MLS teams
├── ncaa/          # Top college teams
├── wnba/          # 12 WNBA teams
├── ufc/           # UFC fighters
├── boxing/        # Boxing promotions/fighters
├── nascar/        # NASCAR drivers/teams
├── tennis/        # ATP/WTA players
└── esports/       # Esports teams
```

## File Naming Convention:
- Use team name in lowercase with hyphens
- Examples: `boston-celtics.svg`, `real-madrid.svg`
- Keep consistent across all leagues

## Image Requirements:
- Format: SVG preferred (PNG as backup)
- Size: 512x512px minimum
- Background: Transparent
- Quality: Official team logos when possible

## Sources:
- Official team websites
- ESPN media library
- Sports reference sites
- Wikipedia (verified logos)

## Priority Teams:
1. **NBA**: All 30 teams
2. **NFL**: All 32 teams
3. **Premier League**: Top 6 + popular teams
4. **La Liga**: Real Madrid, Barcelona, Atletico
5. **NCAA**: Duke, UNC, Kentucky, Kansas, UCLA
